UNDERSTANDING THE RESULT

Why small probabilities matter

01

Fair probability

With six equally likely colors, a particular color has a 1/6 chance of appearing in each box.

02

Three boxes

Three independent boxes produce 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 possible ordered outcomes.

03

Small bias

A small probability difference may be difficult to notice in a few rounds, but becomes easier to observe over many trials.

04

Statistics reveal patterns

Comparing theoretical probability with thousands of actual outcomes helps reveal the underlying distribution.
