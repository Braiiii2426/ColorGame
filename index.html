What's happening?

01

Six possible colors

Each box has six possible outcomes. If every color has equal probability, each color has a 1/6 chance, or about 16.67%, of appearing in one box.

02

Three independent boxes

Three boxes create 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 possible ordered combinations when every box is independent.

03

Random doesn't mean equal results

Over a small number of rounds, results can look very uneven. With many rounds, the percentages generally move closer to the theoretical probabilities.

04

Fair vs. biased simulation

FAIR mode gives all six colors equal probability. BIASED mode is an educational demonstration showing how changing probabilities affects long-term results.
